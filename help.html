A little help getting connected.

Keep your people close, even when your connection has other plans.

Messages and reconnection

Connecting means LYRIA is waiting for your account or conversation. Offline means your browser has lost its network connection. Choose Reconnect when you are online again.

Sent means the server confirmed your message. Not confirmed means the result is uncertain. Keep your draft and retry after reconnecting; LYRIA reuses the original message ID to avoid sending it twice.

Text drafts stay in this browser tab, separately for each account and conversation. Reloading restores them. Closing the tab or clearing browser storage can remove drafts. Signing out clears saved message drafts. Private browsing or full storage may prevent draft backup.

If a cooldown appears, wait for the stated time. Do not repeatedly reload or send. Technical limits apply equally to everyone and help LYRIA stay on its free infrastructure.

Voice and microphone

  1. Open Settings → Voice & devices. Choose your microphone and use Test microphone. The test stops automatically and does not send audio to anyone.
  2. If access is blocked, allow the microphone in your browser’s site settings. On iPhone/iPad, also check Safari’s microphone permissions in device settings.
  3. If the microphone is busy, close other calls and retry. If your device disappears, choose System default or reconnect the microphone.
  4. Use Check connection to test the relay without opening the microphone. A missing TURN relay can make calls fail across phone networks, school Wi-Fi, or restrictive routers even when the microphone works.
  5. After an interruption, choose Join voice again. LYRIA does not automatically reopen your microphone on reload.

Show safe diagnostics in Settings when asking for help. It excludes API keys, account and device IDs, relay credentials, and IP addresses. Never post an entire credentials URL or private conversation.

Blocking and reporting

Open a member’s profile to block or report them, or use Report on a message. Blocking hides their messages and requests, and server rules reject new direct requests and call invitations between you. It does not erase existing history, remove either person from shared conversations, or isolate audio in a shared voice room. Leave a shared call if needed.

Manage blocked members in Settings. Reports include the selected message text when applicable and your optional details. Moderators can review reports; reporting does not automatically punish someone. Do not include passwords or unrelated private information.
